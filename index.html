Favourite (or un-favourite) nades on csnades.gg, then pull them in here. The bookmark asks what to grab: ⭐ Favourites only (fast — just updates your stars and adds new favourites) or 🗺 Everything on my maps (every smoke, molotov, flash and HE on the maps you have favourites on, 1–3 minutes).

One time only: drag this button onto your Chrome bookmarks bar. (Already have an older ⭐ bookmark? Delete it and drag this one — it's newer.)

⭐ Grab csnades favourites

Bookmarks bar hidden? Press Ctrl+Shift+B. Can't drag? Click "Copy bookmark code", then create a new bookmark and paste the code as its URL.

2

Open csnades.gg/favourites (signed in with Steam) and click the ⭐ Grab csnades favourites bookmark. When it says Done, click Copy.

3

Paste it here and apply:

The sheet now shows your new list. To keep it for good, download the updated file and replace your old cheat sheet file with it (it goes to your Downloads folder).

⭐ Your favourites

Click the ☆ next to any nade's name to add it (click ★ again to remove it). Favourites are saved in this browser, so every friend who opens this file keeps their own list.

1

Share your list with a friend: copy it and send it to them (Discord, WhatsApp…). It contains your favourites, your hidden nades and your saved video frames.

2

Use a friend's list: paste what they sent you.

3

Keep them in the file: download a copy of the sheet with your favourites built in. Use it as a backup, or send it to someone so they start with your picks.
